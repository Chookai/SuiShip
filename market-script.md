# 30-Second Market Page Script

This page explains why SuiShip matters commercially.

The pain is already massive: trade-finance fraud costs over 42 billion dollars every year, the global trade-finance gap is 2.5 trillion dollars, and customs clearance can still take five to seven days.

SuiShip addresses this by turning shipment documents into a verifiable passport. AI agents check the documents, MemWal remembers trusted party history, Walrus stores evidence, SEAL protects private data, and Sui anchors the result on-chain.

The market is large: a 54 billion dollar trade and supply-chain software opportunity, with a 9 billion dollar serviceable market for AI and blockchain verification.

SuiShip can earn through per-shipment passport fees, SaaS workspaces, memory APIs, risk scans, and verification by banks or customs.

In short, SuiShip uses a multi-agent workflow to turn messy trade documents into a verifiable shipment passport, making global trade faster, safer, and finance-ready. Looking ahead, x402 can extend that passport into customs clearance: agents could request inspections, unlock verified data, and settle clearance or verification fees automatically as each shipment reaches the border. That means SuiShip is not just digitizing documents; it is laying the foundation for trade infrastructure where compliance, payments, and trust move together in real time.

# Persistent Agent Script

The Persistent Agent keeps watching the shipment after documents are approved.

Here, it polls live AIS data every 10 seconds, tracks vessel progress, and flags when a shipment pauses unexpectedly.

When an issue appears, the agent summarizes the likely cause and recommended action, so operators can respond before the delay becomes a bigger customs or delivery problem.
